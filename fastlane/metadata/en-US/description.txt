Muzik Box is the simple, offline music player that puts YOU in control of your songs — no streaming, no ads getting in the way of the music, no internet required.

Load your own tracks and enjoy them anywhere: on a flight, on the subway, in the mountains, or anywhere the signal drops.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT

• 100% Offline Playback
Play your own music files with zero internet. Your library, your rules.

• Easy Importing
Add songs your way — transfer over Wi-Fi from your computer, download from a URL, or import straight from the Files app. No cables, no hassle.

• Smart Playlists
Build playlists by hand, or create Smart Playlists with rules that fill themselves automatically as your library grows.

• Powerful Equalizer
Fine-tune your sound with a full band equalizer. Boost the bass, sharpen the highs, and make every track sound the way you like.

• Built-in Lyrics
Follow along with synced lyrics right on the Now Playing screen.

• Audio Editor
Trim and cut any track to keep just the part you want — perfect for making clips and ringtones.

• Sleep Timer
Drift off to your favorite songs and let Muzik Box stop playback automatically.

• Clean, Modern Design
A beautiful, distraction-free player with artwork-driven colors and smooth animations.

• Background Playback & Lock Screen Controls
Keep listening with the screen off and control everything from the lock screen and Control Center.

Muzik Box is made for people who own their music and want a fast, private, offline player that just works.
